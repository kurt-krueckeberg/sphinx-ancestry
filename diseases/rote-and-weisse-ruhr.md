# Disease in the 18th and 19th Century

The Petzen burial records from the *Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*
contain instances of local epidemics of the Rote Ruhr and the Weise Ruhr,
red dynsentary and white dysentary.

## Instances of Rote Ruhr

See image 96, the left page, of the *Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784*.
It lists several burial from 1676 in which the cause of death is the Rote
Ruhr, red dysentary.

On the left page of image 96, the "Roten Ruhr" is listed as the cause of
death in 14 of the 18 burial records.
