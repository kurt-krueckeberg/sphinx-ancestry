# The Ancestry of the Krückebergs of Union Township, Adams County, Indiana

## Where the Documentary Evidence Begins

In 1849 the family of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg emigrated from the village of Berenbusch, Schaumburg-Lippe,
and settled in Union township, Adams County, Indiana. The following year the Carl Friedrich Krückeberg family
also left Berenbusch and joined them in Union township.

Two documents pinpoint Berenbusch as the place of origin of these two Krückeberg families:

- A typewritten document with the heading
  {external+genealogy:doc}`Family Record of the Immigrant Carl Friedrich
  Krückeberg <derived-church-records/FS-CFK>`[^family-record], whose original source is unknown.
- The biography of {external+immanuel:doc}`Johann Heinrich
  Krückeberg <immanuel/DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01>` found in the Immanuel Lutheran
  Church (Decatur, Indiana) church register, 1850-1905.[^immanuel-register]

The {external+genealogy:doc}`family record of the immigrant Carl Friedrich
Krückeberg <derived-church-records/FS-CFK>` was certified by the acting
Petzen pastor L. Schwerdtmann from Bergkirchen.  The original source for
the information in the first document is given in its opening sentence:
"Laut Angabe des hiesigen Kirchenbuchs…​" (according to the local
church register). The parish village of **Petzen** is identified at the
bottom of the document:

> Die Übereinstimmung vorstehender Angaben mit den hiesigen
> Kirchenbüchern bescheinigt den Auswanderern unter Anwuenschung
> des göttlichen Segens — sub fide pastorali
>
> **Petzen** d. 23sten September 1850
> Pede vacanti
> der vikarirende Prediger
>
> Fürstentum Schaumburg Lippe.
>
> L. Schwerdtmann von Bergkirchen.

**English translation:**
>
> The foregoing statements are hereby certified as conforming to the local
> church registers and are issued to the emigrants with best wishes for
> God’s blessing, under pastoral authority.
>
> **Petzen**, 23 September 1850.
> The parish being vacant,
> the acting pastor.
>
> Principality of Schaumburg-Lippe.
>
> L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen.
>
> --  translation of the bottom portion of the 'family record of the emigrant Carl Friedrich Krückeberg

Both documents are strictly religous profiles composed soley from the
contents of the Petzen church register records involving the family
members. They serve as an unofficial index for the Petzen parish records
they reference. This is extremely helpful because the Petzen parish
registers contain neither an index nor a table of contents.

## Their Family Relationship

We know that Johann Heinrich Krückeberg, who immigrated to Adams CO Indiana
in 1849, was related to Carl Friedrich Krückeberg, who arrived there the
next year, because he tell us so. While there are many clues to their
familial relationship in the Petzen church records, we can quote Carl
Friedrich Krückeberg himself on this topic. When he petitioned the
Schaumburg-Lippe Rentkammer (fiscal chamber), on September 11, 1850,
requesting to {external+nla:doc}`sell his holding at no. 18 Berenbusch
<146/carl-fried-supplication-to-sell>`, he wrote:

> As I intend to emigrate to America with my entire family, **where several
> relatives of mine already reside** and are doing quite well, I have sold my
> small new-settler holding, No. 18 in Berenbusch, to the shoemaker Wilhelm
> Meier, a native of Evesen, presently residing in Röcke.
>
> --  Carl Friedrich request of Rentkammer to approval sale of his holding

[^family-record]: “Family Record of the Immigrant Carl Friedrich Krückeberg,”
    typescript copy of a family record certified at Petzen, Schaumburg-Lippe,
    23 September 1850; privately held by Kurt Krueckeberg, Fort Wayne, Indiana.
    Kurt Krueckeberg received the copy from his first cousin once removed,
    Melvin Krueckeberg, who had received it from Harry Frederick Krueckeberg,
    a first cousin of Melvin and of Kurt's father. Harry stated in an email
    that he believed his father had prepared the typescript from an unknown
    source. The underlying source and the typescript's authorship remain
    unverified.

[^immanuel-register]: Immanuel Lutheran Church (Decatur, Indiana),
    church register, 1850–1905, biography of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg;
    TIFF digital images on DVD, privately held by Kurt Krueckeberg,
    Fort Wayne, Indiana. Images obtained from the church with the
    assistance of Todd, Janet, and Norma Krueckeberg.
