# FamilySearch Error

On November 26, 1826, Christian Friederich Bleeke, No. 1[3] in Leeteln, married the daughter of the late Jobst Heinrich
Krückeberg, # 10 Bärenbusch. Her name in the marriage record is given as **Luise Eleonore Krückeberg**, and her age is
given as 30 years. But Jobst Heinrich did not have a daughter who would have been 30 years old in 1826. No births
of Krückeberg children are recorded between 1795 and 1797 in the Petzen church register for the years 1784-1827.

Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg (1806-1879) had four children. His {external+genealogy:doc}`death and burial record <petzen/PET-D-1822b>` of Oct. 1822 states that his survivors were: **"a widow and four children, namely three daughters and
one son".** The birth record of his last child and first son, Johann Heinrich, states that he is the **"fourth child
and the first son"**. The four children documented children of Jobst Heinrich according to the Petzen church
records are:

1. {external+genealogy:doc}`Philippine Carolina Christine, born 1 Feb. 1792 <petzen/PET-B-1792a>`
2. {external+genealogy:doc}`Anne Christina Eleonore, born 14 Feb. 1798 <petzen/PET-B-1798a>`
3. {external+genealogy:doc}`Sophie Wilhelmine, born 24 Jan 1803 <petzen/PET-B-1803a>`
4. {external+genealogy:doc}`Johann Heinrich, born 12 Aug 1806 <petzen/PET-B-1806a>`

The age of 30 years old is incorrect, if the bride was a daughter of Jobst Heinrich. Furthermore, her name in the
Frille Kirchenbuchduplikat, the duplicate churvch register that was required to be kept in Westfalen, has a name
that differs from the baptism and confirmation records of Jobst Heinrich’s children. Philippine Carolina Christine
Krückeberg married in 1812 and again in 1834. The only candidate whose name and age are closest to the Frille
marriage record is Anne Christina Eleonore, born 14 February 1798. She would have been 28 years, 10 months, and 12
days old on the date of her marriage.
